What we collect

Account details you give us (email, display name, profile link) and the profile content you choose to publish: photo, headline, contact details and links.

Visitor analytics

When someone opens a profile, Tapit counts the view and its entry path (NFC, QR or direct) in hourly totals. Unique views are estimated without storing who visited.

Profile owners and administrators only ever see aggregate counts.
