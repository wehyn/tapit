Tapit · UI audit · Oct 2026

Every screen, one system.

I audited 26 screens across the public site, sign-in, the customer workspace and the admin workspace. The current palette stays. What changes is structure: one shell for every role, one spacing scale, three radii, seven text sizes, and separation by space and tone instead of divider lines.

Findings

Ranked by how much each one gets in the way of moving around the app.

NavigationSidebarNav, CustomerShell, AdminShell

Two shells, two mental models

Customers get an account menu; admins get a sign-out button. Admin access is a single "Admin workspace" link at the bottom, and going back means finding "My profile" in a third group. Fix: one shell with a Customer / Admin switch at the top.

Navigation1024–1279px

The icon-only rail hides where you are

Between 1024 and 1279px the sidebar collapses to 72px; labels and group names go screen-reader-only. Fix: keep a 248px labelled sidebar on desktop; on phones use a bottom tab bar.

MobileHamburger drawer

Every destination is two taps away

The phone header shows only a ☰ glyph. Profile, Links and Analytics, the three things people do daily, hide in a drawer. Fix: a four-tab bar plus a More sheet.

IA/app/account/build-card

Build card lives under Account

It shows in the Workspace group but the URL nests it under Account, so the breadcrumb and back button disagree with the sidebar. Fix: move it to /app/card under "Share".

StatusDraftSaveContext

Draft and live state are easy to miss

Saving happens on navigation, but nothing on screen says whether changes are saved or whether the profile is live. Fix: a save pill in the top bar and a Live card in the sidebar.

VisualPanel, Button, tables

Lines, outlines and lift everywhere

Each card has a border and a shadow; every button lifts 3px and scales 2.5% on hover; admin tables split rows with lines. Fix: tonal tiles, a single soft shadow, colour-only hover.

TokensProfile themes

About 30 colours live outside the tokens

The Paper, Moss and Night profile themes hard-code hex values (#2c2420, #3e806d, #176b57…). Fix: keep them, but move them into named theme tokens next to the core palette.

OnboardingNavigation tour

A coach-mark tour instead of a checklist

The first-visit spotlight tour explains the menu but not what to do. Fix: a "Finish your profile" checklist on the Profile page that links to each step.
